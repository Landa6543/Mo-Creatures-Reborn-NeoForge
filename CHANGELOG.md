### Mo' Creatures Reborn v1.0.0 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

#### 🎉 Initial Release
Welcome to the first official release of **Mo' Creatures Reborn** for **Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**!

---

#### 🌟 What's New & Included:
- **NeoForge 1.21.1 Port:** Complete port and modernization of the legendary creature ecosystem.
- **60+ Restored Creatures:**
  - Terrestrial wildlife: Big cats (lions, tigers, leopards, panthers), bears, elephants, boars, deer, bunnies, ostriches, mice, moles, and more.
  - Aquatic life: Sharks, dolphins, stingrays, piranhas, and exotic fish species.
  - Mythical & hostile beasts: Wyverns, elemental manticores, ogres, golems, werewolves, and wraiths.
  - Insects & amphibians: Butterflies, crickets, fireflies, snails, turtles, and frogs.

#### 🦅 Mount Physics & Flight System:
- **Balanced Ground Movement:** Ridden mounts now walk and sprint at natural ground speeds, eliminating uncontrollably fast ground physics.
- **Dynamic 3D Flight Controls:** Smooth aerial mechanics for Wyverns, Pegasi, Manticores, and Ostriches:
  - `Space`: Ascend / Gain altitude
  - `Shift`: Descend / Land
  - `Ctrl`: High-speed sprint boost in mid-air

#### 🛠️ Fixes & Polish:
- **Anatomy & Look Animations:** Fixed neck and head bending distributions for Big Cats (Lions) and Manticores to eliminate unnatural head detachment and mane clipping.
- **Authentic Animations:** Restored original bird flight flapping animations.
- **Sound Attenuation:** Fixed spatial audio dropoff for wildlife (turkeys, birds, etc.) to prevent loud sounds from playing across long distances.
- **Pet Naming:** Dedicated pet naming screen fixed and fully functional upon taming.
- **Vanilla Integration:** Spawn eggs are cleanly integrated directly into vanilla creative inventory tabs.

---

#### 📋 Dependencies:
- **Minecraft:** `1.21.1`
- **NeoForge:** `21.1.65+`
- **GeckoLib:** Required
- **Pehkui:** Optional / Recommended
