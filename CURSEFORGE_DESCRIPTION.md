# 🦁 Mo' Creatures Reborn (NeoForge 1.21.1)

![Minecraft](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.21.1-brightgreen)
![NeoForge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-NeoForge-orange)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPL--3.0-blue)

**Mo' Creatures Reborn** is the modern revitalization of the legendary mod originally created by **DrZhark**. Rebuilt and revitalized for **Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge**, it brings back the most vast, diverse, and beloved creature ecosystem in Minecraft history!

---

## ✨ Features

- 🐾 **Dozens of Realistic Wild Animals:** Lions, tigers, bears, elephants, crocodiles, deer, boars, dolphins, sharks, rays, birds, butterflies, insects, and much more!
- 🐉 **Mythical & Fantasy Beasts:** Wyverns, elemental manticores (fire, frost, dark, toxic), golems, ogres, and werewolves.
- 🦅 **Rideable Ground & Flying Mounts:** Tame and ride pegasi, wyverns, ostriches, big cats, and manticores. Features smooth, balanced ground walking and fast aerial flight (Space to ascend, Shift to descend, Ctrl to sprint/boost).
- 🦴 **Taming & Custom Pet Naming:** Tame your favorite companions and name them directly through custom UI dialogs.
- ⚔️ **Armor & Weapon Sets:** Craft elemental scorpion armor and weaponry with unique combat effects, slowness, poison, wither, and full-set bonuses.
- 🥚 **Clean Vanilla Integration:** Spawn eggs are neatly categorized directly into vanilla creative inventory tabs.

---

## 📋 Requirements & Dependencies

To run **Mo' Creatures Reborn**, make sure you have installed:
- **Minecraft:** `1.21.1`
- **Mod Loader:** `NeoForge` (version 21.1.65 or newer recommended)
- **Required Dependencies:**
  - [GeckoLib](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/geckolib) *(Required for models and animations)*
- **Optional Dependencies:**
  - [Pehkui](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/pehkui) *(Optional / Recommended for scale adjustments)*
